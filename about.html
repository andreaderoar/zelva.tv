Director · Editor · Storyteller
Andrea
Founder, Zelva Creative Studio

I studied Digital Design at Vancouver Film School, specializing in motion graphics — and never really stopped moving. Over the past 10+ years I've worked as a freelance editor, cinematographer, and animator, with full-time stints at The Martin Agency and Hue & Cry animation studio, building a craft shaped by one constant obsession.

The story. Always the story.

Today, I create with AI. I combine Adobe Premiere and After Effects with the latest AI video tools — building workflows that are faster, more expressive, and closer to the feeling you're chasing. I run Zelva, an AI video production studio, and I teach people how to harness these tools with intention, taste, and a real point of view.

I stay at the edge of what's possible. New tools, new workflows, new ways to surprise an audience. But the real why never changes.

"Funny stories. Real stories. Inspired ones.
That's what I'm here for."
